## 🎯 How to Upload Your New Headshot (Quick Version)

### Easiest Method: GitHub Mobile App

1. **Download the GitHub app**:
   - iPhone: https://apps.apple.com/app/github/id1477376905
   - Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.github.android

2. **Upload your photo**:
   - Open the app and go to `sande570/sande570.github.io`
   - Navigate to: `assets` → `img`
   - Tap the **"+"** button
   - Select **"Upload files"**
   - Choose your new headshot from your photos
   - **Name it: `prof_pic.jpg`**
   - Tap **"Commit changes"**

3. **Wait and view**:
   - Wait 2-5 minutes for GitHub to rebuild your site
   - Visit https://sande570.github.io
   - Your new picture will appear!
